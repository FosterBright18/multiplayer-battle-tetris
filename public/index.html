How to play

  • ←→Move
  • ↑XRotate clockwise
  • ZRotate counter-clockwise
  • ↓Soft drop
  • SpaceHard drop
  • CShiftHold piece
  • 1QUse power-up slot 1
  • 2EUse power-up slot 2

Clear lines to send garbage. Every 6th piece contains a glowing ★ block. Clear its line to earn a random power-up (you can hold 2).
